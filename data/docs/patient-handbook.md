# Mirador Dental: Patient Handbook

Welcome to Mirador Dental. This handbook explains how the clinic works, from booking your first
appointment to paying for your treatment. Please keep it for reference. If anything here is
unclear, our reception team will be happy to help.

Edition 4, 2026. This handbook replaces all earlier editions.

## About the clinic

Mirador Dental is a general dental practice in the Chamberí district of Madrid. We opened in 2011
and today care for around 4,000 patients of all ages. The clinic has three treatment rooms, a
small recovery room and a reception area on the first floor of a residential building.

Our team:

- Dr. Elena Ruiz Navarro, clinical director and general dentist
- Dr. Tomás Ferrer Gil, general dentist
- Dr. Ana Belén Ortiz, general dentist with a special interest in nervous patients
- Lucía Sanz and Pablo Martín, dental hygienists
- Carmen Díaz, practice manager
- Javier Molina and Sara Ibáñez, reception

All our dentists are registered with the Colegio Oficial de Dentistas de Madrid. Every member of
the clinical team takes part in continuing training each year.

We speak Spanish and English. Dr. Ferrer also speaks French. If you would like to bring an
interpreter or a family member to help you understand your treatment, you are welcome to.

### What we do and what we do not do

We provide general dentistry for adults and children: examinations, cleanings, fillings and
whitening. We do not offer orthodontics (braces or aligners), dental implants, root canal
treatment, wisdom tooth surgery or sedation. If you need one of these treatments, your dentist
will refer you to a trusted specialist and send them a summary of your records with your
permission.

## Opening hours and contact

The clinic is open Monday to Friday, from 09:00 to 13:00 and from 15:00 to 19:00. We close for
lunch between 13:00 and 15:00. We are closed on Saturdays, Sundays and on public holidays in the
Community of Madrid, including local Madrid holidays such as San Isidro on 15 May.

During the week of 24 December to 1 January, and for two weeks in August, the clinic runs a
reduced service. The exact dates are posted at reception and announced by phone message each
year.

### The automated phone assistant

When you call the clinic line, you may first speak with our automated phone assistant. It can
book, move and cancel appointments, and answer common questions about the clinic. Calls with the
assistant are stored, as explained in the privacy section of this handbook. If you would rather
speak to a person, ask the assistant for the reception team, or call during opening hours.

### Urgent dental problems

If you have severe tooth pain, a broken tooth or a lost filling, call the urgent dental line on
910 000 002. During opening hours we keep time free every day for urgent cases, and we will try
to see you the same day.

If you have difficulty breathing or swallowing, heavy bleeding that will not stop, or swelling
that is spreading quickly, do not wait for the clinic. Call 112 straight away.

## Finding us

The clinic is at Calle del Mirador 14, on the first floor. The entrance is next to the pharmacy,
and the clinic bell is marked "Mirador Dental, 1º". Reception will open the door for you.

### By metro and bus

The nearest metro station is Iglesia (Line 1), a five-minute walk away. Quevedo (Line 2) and
Bilbao (Lines 1 and 4) are both about ten minutes on foot. Buses 3, 37 and 149 stop on the
corner of Calle de Santa Engracia, one minute from the door.

### By car

There is no parking at the clinic itself, and street parking in Chamberí is regulated (SER zone),
with green spaces for residents and blue spaces for visitors, limited to four hours. The nearest
public car park is Aparcamiento Santa Engracia, 150 metres away, open 24 hours. At the time of
writing it costs about 3 euros per hour. We do not validate parking tickets.

### By bicycle and taxi

BiciMAD stations are on Calle de Santa Engracia and Glorieta de Quevedo. Taxis can stop in front
of the building for a short time.

### Step-free access

The building has a lift large enough for a standard wheelchair, and there is a step-free route
from the street to the lift. Our ground-floor entrance has a small ramp. One of our three
treatment rooms has extra space for a wheelchair, and our toilet is accessible. Please tell us
when you book if you need this room, so we can make sure it is free.

## Becoming a patient

Everyone new to the clinic starts with a first-visit consultation. This gives the dentist a full
picture of your dental health before any other treatment is planned. Until you have attended your
first visit, the only appointment we can book for you is the first visit itself.

You count as a new patient until you attend your first visit. After that you are a registered
patient, even if you do not come back for a while. Registered patients book check-ups, cleanings,
fillings and whitening directly, and never need another first-visit consultation.

### One phone number, one patient

We use your mobile number to find your records, so each patient needs their own number. If you
call from a number we know, the phone assistant will recognise you and book for you. To book for
a partner, a parent or an adult child, please ask them to call from their own number, or call
reception during opening hours.

Children under 16 are registered by a parent or guardian at reception, and reception books all
their appointments. The phone assistant cannot book for children. A parent or guardian must come
with them to every visit.

## Booking an appointment

You can book an appointment by phone, at reception, or through the phone assistant at any time
of day. Appointments are 30 minutes long, and can be booked from two hours ahead up to 60 days
ahead. If you need several appointments, for example for more than one filling, each one is booked
as its own appointment.

When you book, please tell us:

- your full name, as it appears on your identity document
- what you would like to book
- the days and times that suit you

The phone assistant reads every booking back to you before it confirms it. Nothing is booked
until you say yes.

### Appointment reminders

We send a text message reminder two days before every appointment. Reply "C" to confirm. If you
do not receive reminders, please check that we have your current mobile number.

### Running late

If you are running late, please call us. We can usually still see you if you arrive within 10
minutes of your appointment time. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to move
your appointment to another day, so that other patients are not kept waiting.

## Your first visit

Please arrive 10 minutes before your first-visit appointment to fill in a short health form. You
can also fill it in at home: reception will send you a link when you book.

### What to bring

- your DNI, NIE or passport
- your insurance card, if you have dental insurance
- a list of any medicines you take
- any recent dental x-rays or records from another dentist, if you have them
- your glasses, if you need them for reading forms

### What happens at the first visit

The first-visit consultation takes about 30 minutes. The dentist will:

1. ask about your general health and any dental problems
2. examine your teeth, gums, tongue and the inside of your mouth
3. take x-rays if they are needed and you agree
4. explain what they found, in plain language
5. give you a written treatment plan with prices, if you need any treatment

The first-visit consultation costs 30 euros. It does not include a cleaning or any treatment,
which are booked separately afterwards. If your treatment plan includes several steps, you do not
need to decide anything on the day. You can take the plan home and call us when you are ready.

## Treatments and prices

All our appointments last 30 minutes. Prices include the dentist's or hygienist's time and all
materials, and are the same whichever dentist you see. They do not include laboratory work from
outside the clinic, which we will always quote in advance.

| Treatment | Price | Who it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First-visit consultation | 30 euros | New patients only |
| Check-up | 40 euros | Registered patients |
| Cleaning | 60 euros | Registered patients |
| Filling | 80 euros per filling | Registered patients |
| Whitening | 150 euros | Registered patients aged 18 and over |

### Check-up

A check-up is a routine examination for registered patients. The dentist looks at your teeth,
gums and the soft tissues of your mouth, checks any existing fillings, and tells you whether you
need treatment. Most patients have a check-up once or twice a year. A check-up does not include a
cleaning.

### Cleaning

A cleaning is carried out by one of our hygienists. It removes the hard deposits (tartar) that
brushing cannot, and polishes the teeth. You can book a cleaning on its own or on the same day as
a check-up. Many patients book both together so that they only need to visit once.

### Filling

A filling repairs a tooth that has a cavity or a small break. We use tooth-coloured composite
fillings only. We do not place silver (amalgam) fillings. Each filling is booked as its own
30-minute appointment. If you need several, the dentist will tell you which to treat first.

### Whitening

Our in-clinic whitening lightens the natural colour of your teeth in one appointment. At the
start of the appointment the dentist checks that your teeth and gums are healthy enough for
whitening. If they are not, we treat the problem first and move your whitening to a later date,
at no charge. Whitening is not available for anyone under 18, by law.
It does not change the colour of fillings, crowns or veneers.

### Treatments we refer elsewhere

For orthodontics, implants, root canal treatment, oral surgery and children's specialist care, we
refer you to specialists we know and trust. Referral is free. The specialist will give you their
own prices and booking times.

### Children's appointments

We see children from the age of three. A child's first appointment is a short, friendly visit to
get used to the chair, the light and the team, and the dentist only examines their teeth if the
child is happy to. Children's check-ups and cleanings cost the same as adults'. We book children's
appointments between 15:00 and 17:00 where we can, so that they miss less school, and these times
fill up quickly in September and January.

### Gift vouchers

Gift vouchers for a cleaning or for whitening are available at reception, in any amount from 30
euros. Vouchers are valid for 12 months and can be used by anyone who is a registered patient, or
towards a first-visit consultation for someone new. They cannot be exchanged for cash.

## Paying for your treatment

You pay at reception at the end of each appointment. We accept:

- debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express)
- contactless payment by phone or watch
- Bizum
- cash, in euros

We do not accept cheques. For treatment plans over 400 euros, you can pay in up to three monthly
instalments with no interest. Ask reception to set this up before your treatment starts.

### Receipts and invoices

You receive a receipt by email after every payment. If you need a full invoice (factura) with
your name and tax number, for example to claim from your insurer or your employer, ask at
reception or by email. We can issue invoices for payments made in the last four years.

## Dental insurance

Mirador Dental is a private clinic. We are not part of the Spanish public health service, so we
cannot treat patients through the public system.

We have direct billing agreements with two insurers: Previsora Dental and Salud Clara. If you are
insured with one of them, bring your insurance card and we will bill the insurer directly. You
only pay any part your policy does not cover. Please check your policy before your visit: most
policies cover check-ups and cleanings, but some do not cover whitening.

If you have another dental insurer, you pay us in full, and we give you an itemised invoice to
claim the cost back from your insurer.

### Visitors from other countries

The European Health Insurance Card (EHIC) is not accepted at private clinics in Spain, including
ours. If you are visiting from abroad, you pay in full and can claim from your own travel or
health insurance using our invoice.

## Changing or cancelling an appointment

You can move or cancel an appointment through the phone assistant, by calling reception, or by
email. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice. The phone assistant can change appointments that
are more than 24 hours away. For an appointment within the next 24 hours, please call reception
on 910 000 001.

### Late cancellations and missed appointments

If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, or do not come to your appointment, we may charge
a missed appointment fee of 20 euros. We understand that emergencies happen, and we do not charge
the fee for illness or a family emergency if you let us know. After two missed appointments in a
year without notice, we may ask you to pay in advance when you book.

### If we need to move your appointment

Very occasionally, we have to move an appointment because a dentist is ill. If this happens, we
call you as early as we can and offer you the next available time. You will never be charged when
we move an appointment.

## After your visit

Your dentist or hygienist will tell you anything you need to know before you leave. If you have
any questions after your appointment, call the clinic on 910 000 001 during opening hours. We do
not give medical advice by phone message or email.

### After a cleaning

Your gums may feel a little tender for a day. This is normal and usually settles quickly.

### After a filling

If you had a local anaesthetic, your lip and tongue may feel numb for two to three hours. Take
care not to bite them, and wait until the numbness has worn off before eating or drinking anything
hot. Tooth-coloured fillings are fully set when you leave the clinic.

### After whitening

Some people notice that their teeth are more sensitive for a day or two after whitening. The
dentist will give you a short sheet of aftercare advice on the day.

### If something does not feel right

If you have pain that is getting worse, or a filling feels high when you bite, call us. We will
check it free of charge within 30 days of your treatment.

## Your records and privacy

We keep your dental records securely for as long as the law requires, which in Spain is at least
five years after your last visit. Your records are never shared without your permission, except
when the law requires it.

Calls with our phone assistant are recorded and stored, together with a written summary of the
call, so that we can check what was booked and improve the service. We keep call recordings for
90 days and then delete them, unless they are needed to resolve a complaint.

You have the right to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, and in some cases to
ask us to delete it. To make a request, email privacidad@miradordental.example or ask at reception.
We will reply within one month.

## Hygiene and safety

Every instrument used in your mouth is either single-use or cleaned and sterilised in our
autoclave between patients. Each sterilised pack is sealed and dated, and opened in front of you.
Treatment rooms are cleaned between every patient, and our water lines are tested every month.

If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or have recently started a new medicine, please
tell your dentist before your treatment, even if it seems unrelated to your teeth.

## Feedback and complaints

We want to hear about your experience, good or bad. You can tell any member of the team, email
hola@miradordental.example, or ask reception for our feedback form.

If you want to make a formal complaint, ask the practice manager, Carmen Díaz. We acknowledge every
complaint within two working days and aim to give you a full reply within 15 working days. As
required in the Community of Madrid, official complaint forms (hojas de reclamaciones) are also
available at reception at any time.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I bring my child to my appointment?

Yes, but please bring another adult to look after them if they are under 12, as children cannot
stay alone in the waiting room during your treatment.

### Do you see patients who are nervous about the dentist?

Yes. Dr. Ortiz has a special interest in nervous patients. Tell us when you book, and we will
give you a longer explanation before treatment, and agree a signal you can use to stop at any time.

### Can I have a check-up and a cleaning on the same day?

Yes. They are two separate 30-minute appointments, usually booked one after the other.

### How often should I have a cleaning?

Most patients have one or two cleanings a year. Your hygienist will suggest what suits you.

### Is there Wi-Fi in the waiting room?

Yes. The network name and password are on the card at reception.

### Can I get a copy of my x-rays?

Yes. Ask at reception or by email, and we will send them to you by email within five working
days, free of charge.

### Do you have a toilet for patients?

Yes. It is next to reception, and it is wheelchair accessible.

### Can I pay for my partner's treatment?

Yes. Anyone can pay at reception. The invoice is always issued in the name of the patient who
received the treatment.
